We developed an engaging and educational game designed to raise children's awareness of environmental issues, specifically pollution. The game is set in a camping scenario where players complete interactive tasks such as:

- Cleaning trash from a pond
- Turning off cabin lights to conserve energy
- Extinguishing a campfire safely

A mentor character guides players throughout the game, ensuring an educational and immersive experience.

### Key Features

- _Environmental Awareness_: Educates children on pollution, energy conservation, and water quality.
- _Interactive Learning_: Players actively engage in tasks that reflect real-world environmental challenges.
- _GLOBE Protocol Integration_: Ensures scientifically accurate environmental practices.

### Why It Matters

This project tackles the challenge of low environmental awareness among children by making learning interactive and fun. Educating the younger generation about sustainability empowers them to become responsible stewards of the planet.
